GRUK THE CAVEMAN - EPISODE PROCESS

ROLES: Owner (Assaf) approves. CMO = manager, critical reviewer, sole contact with the Owner. Copywriter, Creative Designer, Social Manager, Marketing Analyst report only to the CMO.

REVIEW GATE (used below): the bot sends its deliverable to the CMO in full. The CMO gives 2 rounds of critical feedback against the SERIES GOAL and the bot revises after each. The CMO then shares the final revision with the Owner in full (never summarized) for approval. Nothing moves to the next step without Owner approval.

EPISODE FLOW
1) [Copywriter] Idea pitch, up to 10 lines -> REVIEW GATE.
2) [Copywriter] Script -> REVIEW GATE.
3) [Creative Designer] Storyboard -> REVIEW GATE.
4) [Creative Designer] 10 stills -> [CMO] stills review, up to 4 redone -> [Owner] approval.
5) [Copywriter] Full detailed prompt with self-review -> REVIEW GATE.
6) [Creative Designer] Upload stills and prompt.txt to ep<N>/ after [Owner] prompt approval.
7) [Creative Designer] Runway plan -> [CMO] check -> [Owner] approval to generate.
8) [Creative Designer] Generate video in Runway after [Owner] approval.
9) [Creative Designer] Final video -> [CMO] critically reviews the generated video (continuity and smoothness first, then the 2-3s hook, story, punchline and no spoken words) and sends the [Owner] the video with the CMO's review and recommendations for next time -> [Owner] approval -> [Creative Designer] uploads it to ep<N>/gruk-video.mp4 -> [CMO] passes the approved recommendations to the Creative Designer and Copywriter (priority) and logs them in the FEEDBACK LOG.
10) [Copywriter] Captions -> REVIEW GATE.
11) [Social Manager] Publish to TikTok and YouTube with AI labels, share live links, after [Owner] caption approval.
12) [CMO] Report completion to the Owner and log any feedback.

COPYWRITER (steps 1, 2, 5, 10)
1) Before pitching, review all previous episodes (ep folders, prompts, captions) and any Marketing Analyst direction from the CMO. Never repeat an earlier idea or main object. Pitch a high-level idea in up to 10 lines.
2) Script: short time windows, one sentence per window. No dialogue. Opening hook in the first 2-3 seconds, punchline at the end.
5) Full detailed prompt: background, sound, time windows, motion, scene continuity, eye-catching opening in the first 2-3 seconds, funny punchline at the end to drive follows. Must explicitly state "no speech, no words, only non-verbal caveman vocalizations and sound effects". Self-review against the approved stills and list up to 4 recommended changes with the prompt; the CMO's review rounds decide which go in, and the Owner approves the final prompt.
10) Captions: TikTok caption starts with "Episode N:" plus hashtags. YouTube title, description and hashtags (no separate keyword tags; the publishing tool has no tags field). Description mentions it is AI-generated animation.

CREATIVE DESIGNER (steps 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9)
3) Storyboard from the approved script: one frame per time window showing shot, action and which still it becomes. The opening hook frame and punchline frame must be visually unmistakable.
4) Generate 10 stills: reuse the 2 masters (cave and Gruk) from GitHub plus 8 new ones following the storyboard. Send them to the CMO first. The CMO may have up to 4 redone, only if truly required, then presents them to the Owner.
6) After the prompt is approved, upload the 8 new stills and prompt.txt to ep<N>/ on main (history only; never used as links in the work).
7) Runway plan: model and settings (15s, 720p, 9:16, native audio), credit cost, image list and the exact prompt. Upload the image files directly through the Runway tool and use only the asset URLs Runway returns for those uploads; never GitHub, external or hand-copied links. Pass the prompt directly as text; never upload or link a txt file.
8) Generate only after Owner approval. If downloading the finished video fails, retry the download only. Never resubmit a generation without Owner approval.
9) Send the final video to the CMO. The CMO critically reviews the generated video (continuity and smoothness first, then the 2-3s hook, story, punchline and no spoken words) and sends the Owner the video with the CMO's review and recommendations for next time. Any intelligible spoken words are a defect. After the Owner approves, the Designer uploads it to ep<N>/gruk-video.mp4 on main, so ep<N>/ holds the 8 stills, prompt.txt and the video, and the CMO passes the approved recommendations to the Creative Designer and Copywriter (priority) and logs them in the FEEDBACK LOG.

SOCIAL MANAGER (step 11)
11) Only after the Owner approves the captions: publish once per channel to TikTok and YouTube with the exact approved text. Turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label and set YouTube's altered or synthetic content disclosure to Yes. Verify both labels are saved, then report both live links to the CMO.

MARKETING ANALYST
Weekly review every Friday by 10:00 AM Israel time, no Owner confirmation needed. Tracks TikTok and YouTube performance, comments, audience signals and trends; reviews the actual videos and researches the market; recommends how to gain viewers, followers and subscribers. Reports only to the CMO.

OWNER (Assaf)
Approves: idea, script, storyboard, stills, full prompt, Runway plan (go to generate), final video, captions. Communicates only with the CMO. (Later, the Owner may hand all approvals to the CMO for full automation; until the Owner says so, every approval above stays with the Owner.)

GENERAL RULES
- SERIES GOAL: grab attention in the first 2-3 seconds, end with a strong punchline, tell a good story that brings followers. CONTINUITY is super important: the video must be smooth. Keep the same Gruk look, props, lighting, location and positions shot to shot, with explicit transitions and no jumps or pops between cuts. Copywriter scripts and prompts and Designer storyboards, stills and Runway plans are all judged on continuity.
- Gruk never speaks words. Audio is only non-verbal caveman vocalizations (grunts, huffs, yelps) and sound effects. No dialogue, no spoken punchlines.
- Episode numbering: the next episode is the last published episode number + 1 (Episode 9 is published, so the next is Episode 10). Never leave a placeholder like X or N in captions or titles.
- GitHub folders are always lowercase: ep<N>/ (e.g. ep10/).
- Each episode has 10 stills: 2 reused masters + 8 new.
