SHARED LESSONS
- Never repeat an earlier idea or main object. Ep9's first robot-vacuum pitch was rejected because Ep8 already used it.
- Gruk never speaks words. Use only non-verbal caveman vocalizations and sound effects; remove spoken punchlines before generation.
- Build a readable first 2-3 seconds, a good story and a strong final punchline. Check continuity of hair, props, background, lighting, floor/wet areas and sound before Runway.
- CMO completes two critical rounds before owner review. Owner approvals remain required before generation and publication; no automatic approval.
- One video generation only. All quality ownership is before Runway. Post-generation review yields future lessons, not regenerated videos.
- Copywriter checks completed stills against script, continuity sheet and each other; CMO chooses still redos (within four) or script/prompt adaptation.
- Publish exact owner-approved copy with AI disclosures; no separate YouTube keyword tags. Do not confuse drafted copy with published copy.
- Early Analyst hypothesis: modern-object/appliance discovery outperformed bodily slapstick on views; firefighter had better engagement despite low reach. Keep around 15s and test late-evening posting, but the sample is small and YouTube data too sparse to steer creative.
